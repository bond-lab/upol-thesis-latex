# Formal requirements for BA/MA/PhD theses at UP Olomouc (focus: FF UP linguistics)

Research date: 2026-10-05. Every source is cited by URL; the file names in the
tables are those of the downloaded copies, which are not included in this repository.
Everything below comes from the cited documents. Where a requirement is **not found**, I say so explicitly.

Key terminology: VŠKP = vysokoškolská kvalifikační práce; BP = bakalářská práce
(at FF often "bakalářská diplomová práce"); DP = (magisterská) diplomová práce;
NS = normostrana (1 NS = 1 800 characters incl. spaces, per KAA and KB).

rules (margins, fonts, spacing, title-page layout). FF-B-23/08 only fixes **minimum

and the foreign-language procedure. Typography is left to departments, so a FF class
needs per-department presets.

## 2. University- and faculty-level rules (apply to all FF linguistics programmes)

### 2.1 SZŘ UP (A-10/2011-ÚZ06), čl. 26
- "Závěrečná práce" = bakalářská, diplomová, disertační (26.1).
- A **faculty internal norm sets the required parts (náležitosti)** of each thesis type (26.2).
- Plagiarism definition now **explicitly includes AI-generated text** presented as the
  author's own (26.3, 2025 text).
- The student must consent to non-commercial public access through the electronic records
  system (26.6). BP and DP each get two reviews, one by the supervisor and one by the
  opponent (26.7). Reviews must be available at least 1 week before the defence (26.8).

### 2.2 R-B-17/08 (2017 original)
- On topic assignment, the student enters in STAG: **Czech title, English title,
  supervisor, "Zásady pro vypracování", recommended literature** (čl. 2.3).
- On submission, the student uploads the electronic version to IS/STAG and fills in the
  metadata (čl. 3.2), which the annotation page should mirror:
  a) title in the thesis language, b) English title, c) parallel title and subtitle (if on
  the title page), d) annotation in the thesis language, e) keywords in the thesis language,
  f) English annotation, g) English keywords, h) loose appendices, i) bound appendices,
  j) extent of the thesis, k) language of the thesis.
- The 2017 text still requires **at least two printed copies** plus the electronic version
  (čl. 3.2). FF practice has since changed: under the ÚZ01 consolidation (per the FF FAQ),
  print is needed only if the department demands it.
- STAG upload: **max. file size 60 MB** (Příloha 3, step 6). **No file format is stated**
  (no PDF/A requirement).
- All theses are checked in **theses.cz** for plagiarism (čl. 3.1). They are published in
  the STAG "Kvalifikační práce" database at least 5 working days before the defence (čl. 4.1).
- Rigorous theses (rigorózní) put items a)–k) on a special sheet at the end (čl. 3.2). This
  is not required for BP/DP.

### 2.3 FF-B-23/08 (FF UP dean's norm, effective 16 Sep 2023)
- Čl. 4: topic in STAG during the **2nd year of BA** / **1st year of NMgr**. In maior–minor
  combinations the thesis is written in the maior programme. Team projects are possible.
- Čl. 6, **foreign-language thesis**: allowed "where useful". It must be declared as
  foreign-language already in the STAG *Podklad pro zadání*. The supervisor must be able to
  supervise in that language, and the guarantor must set up a committee competent in it.
- Čl. 7.3, **length**: "Bakalářská práce má rozsah **minimálně 30 normostran**. Diplomová
  práce má rozsah **minimálně 60 normostran**. Součástí VŠKP je **titulní strana, prohlášení
  autora o původnosti zpracování a anotace**. Rozsah VŠKP se počítá bez příloh s daty a
  texty, které nejsou výsledkem samostatné práce studenta." Other forms (exhibition,
  project, article) are possible with approval.
- Čl. 7.5: reviews assess, among other things, the "formální, jazyková, stylistická a
  grafická stránka".
- Čl. 11.2: the student may use a printed copy at the defence.

### 2.4 Electronic submission at FF (FAQ and department pages)
Upload the file to IS STAG and fill in "Doplnit údaje…" (including annotations). Then email
the completed form as a scanned PDF from the university address to the study officer
(studijní referentka). The thesis counts as submitted when the officer confirms. "Student
není povinen práci tisknout" (KAS). KOL: "nevyžaduje tištěnou práci". KB, however,
requires **one printed copy** brought to the department by the day of the defence.
**No PDF/A requirement found anywhere at FF** (also searched web-wide on upol.cz). PdF
accepts .doc/.docx/.pdf.

### 2.5 Visual identity (JVS UP manual, 2017)
- The manual **contains nothing on theses or title pages** (searched for
  závěreč/kvalifik/diplom/bakalář/disert: no hits).
- Elements available: the UP **znak** (shield logo) and **logotypy**. Each faculty has its
  own logotype. The FF pack contains `UP_logo_FF_{stred,horizont}_{cz,en}`,
  `UP_logo_FF-UP_horizont_*`, colour, black and white variants, as PDF (print) and PNG (web).
- Colours: UP blue C100 M38 Y0 K15 / Pantone 2384 / #006FAD. UP grey #5D5E60.
  **FF colour C70 M10 Y0 K0 / Pantone 298 / RGB 58,176,225 / #3AB0E1**.
- Font: **Dederon Sans** (by Tomáš Brousil; commercial) for names, titles and headings.
  Recommended companions: Arial (screen) and Georgia (print), "jejich použití není závazné".
- In practice, department templates use the black UP shield (KOL docx, KAA template) or the
  blue one (KI kidiplom), and set everything else in the body font. None uses Dederon Sans.

## 3. Department requirements (linguistics-relevant)

### 3.1 Katedra asijských studií (KAS), current 2023 version
**Length:** BA 30–40 NS, MA 60–80 NS ("kvantita na úkor kvality není žádoucí!!!").

**Order of pages and mandatory parts (2023):**
1. **Title page** (counted in pagination, number not printed):
   ```
   UNIVERZITA PALACKÉHO V OLOMOUCI
   Filozofická fakulta
   Katedra asijských studií
   BAKALÁŘSKÁ/MAGISTERSKÁ DIPLOMOVÁ PRÁCE
   Název diplomové práce (např. Čínský čaj)
   Název diplomové práce v anglickém jazyce (např. Chinese tea)
   OLOMOUC, rok obhajoby, autor (např. OLOMOUC 2002 Josef Novák)
   vedoucí diplomové práce: ……
   ```
   (The 2021/22 version has "vedoucí diplomové práce" twice, apparently for a
   second supervisor or consultant. The 2023 version has it once.)
2. **Prohlášení o samostatnosti** (counted, number not printed): "Prohlašuji, že jsem
   diplomovou práci vypracoval/a samostatně a uvedl/a veškeré použité prameny a
   literaturu." + "Olomouc, datum". (The 2021/22 version also demanded a handwritten
   signature: "vlastnoruční podpis!!!". The 2023 version does not mention a signature.)
3. **Annotation of about 10 lines** containing: aims, how the aims were met, **keywords**,
   summary of results, and a quantitative summary (**number of pages, number of characters,
   number of bibliography titles, number of appendices**, etc.).
4. Acknowledgements (counted, number not printed).
5. Table of contents.
6. List of figures, graphs, schemes, tables (if many).
7. List of abbreviations, symbols (if many non-standard ones).
8. **Ediční poznámka** (editorial note on transcription, Chinese/Japanese characters).
9. Úvod.
10. Body (chapters/subchapters), including *Materiály a metody* and *Výsledky*.
11. Závěr.
12. **Resumé in English (100–200 words).**
13. List of primary sources (if many).
14. Bibliography (seznam literatury).
15. List of appendices (if any).
16. Appendices (optional unless required by the assignment).

**Formatting (2023):** Times New Roman 12 pt; **margins 25 mm**; line spacing **1.5**;
justified; paragraph indent **1 cm**; pagination "see náležitosti" (front matter counted
but unnumbered).

**Older 2021/22 version (now superseded) differed in:** one-/two-sided printing on white A4.
Margins **left 35 mm, others 25 mm** (for one-sided printing). Cover (desky): UNIVERZITA
PALACKÉHO V OLOMOUCI / Filozofická fakulta / Katedra asijských studií / DIPLOMOVÁ PRÁCE /
year + author. Spine: year + author, bottom to top. BA: 2 printed copies (1 hardbound) plus
a CD with RTF+PDF. The printed *Podklad pro zadání* was to be inserted loose, not bound.
These print/binding rules are obsolete now that submission is electronic only.

**Citation style:** **not specified** by KAS.
**Language:** not specified by KAS beyond the FF-B-23/08 rules. The English title and an
English resumé are mandatory.
**Deadlines 2026/27 (KAS harmonogram):** upload to STAG by 7 Dec 2026 (January exams),
13 May 2027 (June), and 24 Jun 2027 or 1 Jul 2027 (August; the PDF lists both dates).

### 3.2 Katedra obecné lingvistiky (KOL)
From https://kol.upol.cz/studium/diplomove-prace/:
- Submission **electronic only**. Thesis language **Czech, Slovak or English**.
- Length (without appendices): **MA ≥ 108 000 characters (60 NS); BA ≥ 72 000 characters
  (40 NS)**. KOL's BA minimum is therefore stricter than the faculty minimum of 30 NS.
- Minimum number of sources is set by the supervisor. **Citation: "jakékoli standardní
  citační normy"** (any standard style).
- Mandatory structure: declaration of authorship, structured table of contents,
  **annotation in Czech and English of at least 900 characters**, keywords.
- Software is allowed as a thesis form (with printed user documentation). Translation or
  edition is not allowed unless explicitly offered by the supervisor.
- **Word template** `DP-KOL_temp.docx` (2014) contains, in order:
  - *Title page*: "Filozofická fakulta Univerzity Palackého v Olomouci" / "Katedra obecné
    lingvistiky" / black UP shield image / "Název práce" / "magisterská diplomová práce /
    bakalářská diplomová práce" / "Autor: titul, jméno příjmení" / "Vedoucí práce:
    titul(y), jméno příjmení, titul(y)" / "Olomouc" / "rok".
  - *Prohlášení*: "Prohlašuji, že jsem bakalářskou/magisterskou diplomovou práci „Název
    diplomové práce" vypracoval/a samostatně a uvedl/a jsem veškerou použitou literaturu a
    veškeré použité zdroje." / "V … dne … Podpis".
  - *Abstrakt* (CZ): Název práce; Autor práce; Vedoucí práce; Počet stran a znaků; Počet
    příloh; Abstrakt (minimálně 900 znaků); Klíčová slova.
  - *Abstract* (EN): Title; Author; Supervisor; Number of pages and characters; Number of
    appendices; Abstract (900 characters); Keywords.
  - *Obsah*, then *Literatura a zdroje* ("Můžete využít kteroukoli ze standardních
    citačních norem").
  - Page setup: A4, Word default margins (top/bottom 2.54 cm, left/right 3.17 cm),
    12 pt, theme font Cambria. These look like Word defaults rather than an intentional
    spec. **No margin, font or spacing rules are stated on the KOL page.**

### 3.3 Katedra anglistiky a amerikanistiky (KAA)
- Length: BA ≥ 30 NS (1 NS = 1 800 characters incl. spaces), MA ≥ 60 NS (citing the old
  FF-B-21/03, same numbers as FF-B-23/08).
- Required: title page, declaration of authorship. Optional: acknowledgements. Then table
  of contents, text, **annotation**, **resumé in another language** (Czech if the thesis is
  in English, and vice versa), bibliography.
- Students must **not tick "TeX"** in the STAG form. Topics with an unformatted
  bibliography are rejected.
- **Model (linguistics) `Template_BP.pdf`:**
  - *Title page*, centred Times: UNIVERZITA PALACKÉHO V OLOMOUCI / FILOZOFICKÁ FAKULTA /
    KATEDRA ANGLISTIKY A AMERIKANISTIKY / black UP shield / **NÁZEV PRÁCE** (bold) /
    *Bakalářská práce* (bold). Bottom left: Autor: …, Vedoucí práce: …. Centred:
    Olomouc 20XX.
  - *Prohlášení*: "Prohlašuji, že jsem bakalářskou práci na téma XXX vypracoval(a)
    samostatně, a že jsem uvedl(a) úplný seznam citované a použité literatury. V Olomouci
    dne … Podpis".
  - Then: Poděkování (optional) → numbered ToC → chapters → Conclusion → **Resumé** (in
    the other language, about 1 800–2 400 characters) → References → **Anotace** (may also
    be placed before the ToC).
  - *Anotace* fields: Jméno a příjmení; Katedra; Vedoucí práce; Rok obhajoby; Název práce;
    Název práce v angličtině; Přílohy vázané v práci (počet); Počet stran; Počet znaků;
    Jazyk práce; Abstrakt v jazyce práce (about 600 characters); Klíčová slova v jazyce
    práce (about 5–10).
  - References in the linguistics model look like Chicago author-date, e.g. "Chomsky,
    Noam. 1995. *The Minimalist Program*. MIT Press." The linguistics template does not
    state a style explicitly.
- **Literature-section style sheet** (Template_BPLitSection, which says it "applies
  universally to everyone"): Times New Roman. Text 12 pt; headings 14 pt bold. Indent all
  paragraphs except the first after a heading. No gaps between paragraphs. Line spacing
  1.5. Block quotes for quotations over 3 lines, at 11 pt. **Chicago Manual of Style 18th
  ed., Notes and Bibliography.**

### 3.4 Katedra bohemistiky (KB), "Formální podoba…" (Apr 2026)
- **Length:** BA **72 000–108 000 characters (40–60 NS)**, MA **108 000–162 000 (60–90
  NS)**, counted from the introduction to the conclusion, **including footnotes**, without
  appendices. Theses outside the range are not admitted to the defence.
- **Title page must contain:** university, faculty, department; Czech title; English
  title; thesis type (bakalářská / magisterská diplomová práce); author; **study
  programme (studijní obor)**; supervisor; **place and year of submission**.
- **Declaration:** e.g. "Prohlašuji, že jsem tuto diplomovou práci vypracoval/a
  samostatně a uvedl/a v ní všechny použité zdroje a literaturu." + "V Olomouci dne…" +
  **handwritten signature**. It must also affirm originality of content.
- Acknowledgements optional. ToC structured and numbered. **Pages up to and including the
  ToC are not numbered.** Appendices may be numbered separately.
- **Typography:** a plain readable font (Times, Garamond, Arial, Calibri…). Body 12 pt,
  1.5 spacing, justified. Footnotes 10 pt, spacing 1–1.5, **ragged right**. Headings about
  14–16 pt. **Margins: inner/left about 4 cm; outer, top and bottom about 2–3 cm.**
  Czech typographic rules: no line ending in a non-final abbreviation, title or initial;
  non-breaking number+unit; 15 % vs 15%; Czech quotes „…“; en dash in ranges. Quotations
  in quotation marks, **not italics**; italics for titles of works.
- **Anotace** placed **after the conclusion, before the bibliography**. Fields: author
  surname and first name; department and faculty; title; supervisor; **number of
  characters** (text only, intro to conclusion, including footnotes); number of appendices;
  **number of bibliography titles**; keywords (5–10); annotation (5–10 lines, about 600
  characters).
- **Resumé:** mandatory, in a foreign language (English recommended), **1 800–2 400
  characters**.
- **Citation:** **ISO 690**, always with a specific page, even for paraphrases. Repeated
  citations use a short form "Příjmení, Jméno: Dvě slova z názvu…, s. X."
- Submission: STAG upload **plus one printed copy** to the department by the day of the
  defence (kept in the archive).

### 3.5 Germanistika (comparison)

## 4. BA vs MA vs PhD at FF UP

- **BA vs MA** differ at FF level only in **minimum length (30 vs 60 NS)**, topic timing
  (2nd year BA vs 1st year NMgr), and supervisor qualification (supervisor must hold at
  least one degree higher than the thesis type in academic programmes; FF-B-23/08 čl. 4.5).
  Departments add ranges (see matrix). Title-page wording differs: "bakalářská diplomová
  práce" vs "magisterská diplomová práce" (KAS, KOL, KB) or "Bakalářská práce" (KAA).
- **PhD (disertační práce)**, per SZŘ čl. 44 (ÚZ06, 2025):
  - Language: Czech or Slovak, or another language if the accreditation provides for it
    (with prior consent of the oborová rada).
  - Usual parts: state of the art with references; aims; theoretical background; own
    solution; original results and their publication; evaluation of contribution; list of
    sources; **special declaration on intellectual property / copyright in the
    introduction**; **annotation, usually in English or another world language**.
  - Indicative length and other conditions are set by the **oborová rada**.
  - A collection of papers is possible if the faculty allows it.
  - Projects with several researchers need a statement of the student's contribution.
  - **Autoreferát** (same language, plus a 1-page English abstract; a Czech/Slovak summary
    if written in another language) is required where public access is deferred.
  - FF practice: **one printed copy** plus upload to the Portal, with the autoreferát
    uploaded as an attachment. FF stresses the "mandatory foreign-language resumé in the
    prescribed extent" in the dissertation and autoreferát.
  - **No FF-level typographic rules for dissertations found.** Students starting from
    1 Sep 2025 have only the defence as the state exam (no oral state doctoral exam).

## 6. Implications for a FF UP LaTeX class (design notes, not requirements)

- **Fixed at FF level:** a title page, a declaration of originality, an annotation, and
  minimum length. Everything else should be a **department preset** (`dept=kas|kol|kaa|kb`)
  with overridable keys (margins, font, spacing, citation style, annotation position).
- **Common metadata** (from R-B-17/08 čl. 3.2 and dept annotation pages): title (thesis
  language), English title, subtitle, parallel title, author, supervisor (with degrees),
  department, faculty, programme, thesis type (BA/MA/PhD), year of defence/submission,
  place, thesis language, abstract + keywords in thesis language and in English, number of
  pages, **number of characters**, number of bibliography titles, number of bound/loose
  appendices.
- **Automation:** a class could auto-count pages (lastpage) and bibliography items. A
  **character count** would need an external tool such as texcount or pdftotext+wc,
  because the departments define it differently: KB counts intro to conclusion including
  footnotes; FF excludes appendices.
- **KAS needs CJK** for the ediční poznámka and examples, so XeLaTeX/LuaLaTeX with fontspec
  is the better engine choice, unlike the pdfLaTeX-only kidiplom.
- **Signature line** in the declaration: KB requires a handwritten signature; KOL and KAA
  show "Podpis"; KAS 2023 says nothing. Under electronic-only submission this would
  presumably be a scanned or blank line. This is not regulated.
- **Logo:** the UP shield (black or FF-coloured) or the FF logotype from `FF_logotypy.zip`.
  The JVS manual imposes no thesis-specific rules.

## 7. Gaps / not found

- **R-B-17/08-ÚZ01** (consolidated text) could not be downloaded (401/404), so its exact
  changes are unverified. FF pages say it removes the obligatory print copy.
- **No faculty-wide FF UP typographic rules** (page size, margins, fonts, spacing,
  numbering) and **no FF thesis template**.
- **No PDF/A** or other file-format requirement at UP or FF (only the 60 MB limit;
  PdF allows doc/docx/pdf).
- **No binding/printing rules** in force at FF for BA/MA (electronic only). KB requires
  1 printed copy; PhD requires 1 printed copy. Binding type for these is not specified.
  (Old KAS rules on hardback, spine and CD are superseded.)
- **No one-/two-sided rule** in current documents (old KAS: "jednostranný/oboustranný").
- **KAS:** no citation style, no font size for footnotes or headings, no explicit page
  numbering position, no English-language version of the requirements found.
- **KOL:** no typographic rules. The template is from 2014 and its fields differ slightly
  from the web page (e.g. "Počet stran a znaků").
- **KAA linguistics:** citation style not named explicitly (the model looks like Chicago
  author-date). Margins not stated.
- **PhD at FF:** no formatting rules; content follows SZŘ čl. 44, and length/conditions
  come from the individual oborová rada (not researched per programme).
- **Official FF declaration wording for AI use:** none. ai.upol.cz gives principles only.
- **Dederon Sans** (UP corporate font) is not freely distributable; its licence for
  student use was not found.
